# Authorship record

Upstream systemd's contribution policy (docs/CONTRIBUTING.md, "Policy on the use of
Large Language Models (LLMs) and AI tooling") permits AI-assisted contributions but
forbids crediting an AI as author or co-author, or in any way that suggests ownership.
Contributions submitted from this account comply with that policy: the commits that go
upstream carry no AI trailers, and Pétur Ingi Egilsson owns them, answers for them and
can explain them.

This branch is the record of who actually wrote what. It is kept here, in the open,
rather than hidden in anyone else's history. It claims no ownership and no rights; it
states facts.

For each upstream contribution: what was written, which model wrote it, what the human
did, what was verified and what was not.
